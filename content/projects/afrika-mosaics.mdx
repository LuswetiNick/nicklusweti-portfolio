## Project overview

Afrika Mosaics is a content-led portfolio for Kenyan mosaic artist Githaka Karuri. It brings finished work, commissions, writing, and learning material into one visual system without letting the interface compete with the art.

Sanity gives the artist a structured editing experience, while the public site turns that content into responsive galleries and editorial pages that remain coherent across screen sizes.

## Challenges

### One model for varied content

Artwork, articles, courses, and commission information each need different fields and presentation rules. The Sanity content model keeps those differences clear while preserving shared information such as imagery, descriptions, and publishing state.

### Image-led performance

Mosaic work depends on detail and color, so aggressive cropping or slow galleries would undermine the portfolio. The image system balances responsive delivery with deliberate aspect ratios and a visual hierarchy that gives each artwork room to breathe.

### Editorial control without visual drift

The site needed to stay consistent even as new work is published. Reusable page patterns and typed content queries constrain the presentation enough to protect the design while leaving the artist in control of the content.
