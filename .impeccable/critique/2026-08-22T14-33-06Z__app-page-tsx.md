## Overall Impression

The page looks like an authored portfolio and creates a strong first impression, but it weakens exactly where evaluation becomes action. The largest opportunity is to turn visual confidence into recruiter confidence: let project evidence lead earlier, make it legible without hover, and replace the false contact workflow with an honest interview-oriented path.

## What’s Working

1. **The hero identifies Nick immediately.** Name, role, Nairobi, React/Next.js/TypeScript specialization, availability, and the project jump create unusually high identity density without card clutter.

2. **The visual system has a real point of view.** Warm white on uninterrupted midnight, compressed display typography, scarce green signals, hairline structure, and asymmetric staging form a coherent design language.

3. **The page uses real evidence.** Three confirmed case studies, project-specific screenshots, descriptive alt text, and technical stacks build credibility without fabricated metrics. Reduced-motion handling is consistently present.

## Priority Issues

### 1. [P1] The primary conversion control is a false affordance

**Why it matters:** “Send Message” promises delivery, but `onSubmit` always prevents default and produces no state. This can lose the interview inquiry the portfolio exists to generate.

**Fix:** Connect a real delivery path with validation, loading, success, and error states, or remove the editable form for now. The honest interim version should promote a clear `mailto:` action and explicitly state that direct email is the working route.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`

### 2. [P1] Project proof is interaction-hidden and weakest on mobile

**Why it matters:** Desktop summaries and stacks live behind hover/focus overlays. Mobile removes the overlay entirely, leaving only a cropped screenshot and title. Recruiters cannot compare contribution, challenge, or stack efficiently.

**Fix:** Give every project an always-visible evidence caption with category, contribution or technical challenge, two or three key technologies, and an explicit case-study link. Use art-directed crops or `contain` where interface completeness matters; treat hover overlays as enhancement only.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable layout`

### 3. [P1] The recruiter-first goal disappears at the decision point

**Why it matters:** The header advertises availability, but the contact section speaks only to project clients, the selector has no job-opportunity path, and “Get Now” does not name an outcome.

**Fix:** Lead with “Hiring for a frontend role?” and make client work secondary. Rename the fixed CTA to “Email Nick” or “Discuss a role.” If the form later becomes functional, make “Frontend role / interview” the first inquiry type.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`

### 4. [P2] Generic capability claims arrive before the strongest evidence

**Why it matters:** The natural sequence is Hero → About → five Services → Projects. Experience mode should let the work lead; the current order asks visitors to accept claims before seeing proof.

**Fix:** Move Projects directly after the hero or About section. Compress Services into three evidence-backed capability clusters linked to the projects that demonstrate them.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable distill`

### 5. [P2] Desktop service explanations are pointer-dependent

**Why it matters:** Descriptions start at `opacity: 0` and rely on hover. The service `<article>` contains no focusable control, so `focus-within` cannot reveal the copy for keyboard users.

**Fix:** Keep concise descriptions visible at rest, or make each row a real focusable link with a destination and visible focus treatment. Do not gate informational content behind pointer hover.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable adapt`

## Minor Observations

- “Project” should be plural in floating and footer navigation.
- Section metadata uses Inter although the design record assigns Geist Mono to labels.
- Hero stack, edition metadata, and scroll cue reach extremely small sizes and low opacity; important technical evidence should not sit around 9px.
- Infinite project floating is decorative and makes screenshots less stable to inspect.
- Direct email is a valuable fallback and should be more prominent while the form is nonfunctional.
- Studio IO and Resonix are both dark-on-dark screenshots and need stronger framing or art direction to separate them from the midnight canvas.
