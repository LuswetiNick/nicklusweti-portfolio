# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The portfolio serves two audiences:

- Employers and recruiters evaluating Nick Lusweti for software development roles; this is the priority audience.
- Prospective collaborators evaluating Nick for selected contract or product work.

Visitors typically arrive while comparing candidates or collaborators. Their job is to understand Nick's capabilities, inspect credible project evidence, and decide whether to start a conversation. The primary success outcome is a job interview.

## Product Purpose

Present Nick Lusweti's software development work and capabilities through concise technical evidence and detailed project case studies. The portfolio should make his experience easy to evaluate and give qualified visitors a direct route to contact him.

## Positioning

Nick is a Nairobi-based software developer building production web products end to end. React and Next.js interface development remain visible strengths within broader demonstrated work spanning typed application APIs, relational data, authentication, payments, CMS architecture, metering, and cloud storage.

## Operating Context

Visitors review the portfolio on the web, scan the home page for role fit and technical capabilities, open individual project case studies for implementation evidence, and use the contact section when they want to continue the conversation. Project links may also take them to public source repositories and live deployments.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The current portfolio is a responsive web application built with Next.js, React, TypeScript, and MDX.
- It presents an introduction, background, technical capabilities, project gallery, individual project case studies, and contact details.
- The contact form is currently presentational. It is expected to become functional in future work; future interfaces must not imply successful delivery until a real submission path exists.
- Core capability terminology includes web product development; APIs, data, and integrations; and content and learning systems.
- The public location is Nairobi, Kenya.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve “Nick Lusweti” as the public-facing name. “Nicholas Lusweti” may remain where the full legal or copyright form is appropriate.
- Preserve the identity of Nick as a Nairobi-based software developer.
- Preserve React and Next.js expertise as a central strength without presenting Nick as frontend-only.
- Keep the voice direct, credible, technically specific, and grounded in demonstrated work.

## Evidence on Hand

- Three confirmed project case studies: Studio IO, Afrika Mosaics, and Resonix.
- Project summaries and implementation challenges in `content/projects/`.
- Project metadata, technology lists, GitHub links, and live deployment links in `lib/projects.ts`.
- Project imagery in `public/projects/`.
- Service imagery in `public/services/`.
- Public GitHub profile: `https://github.com/LuswetiNick`.
- Public contact email: `luswetideveloper@gmail.com`.
- No testimonials, client logos, hiring outcomes, performance benchmarks, or other third-party endorsements are currently present; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Lead visitors from quick role recognition to concrete project evidence.
2. Prioritize the information employers and recruiters need to justify an interview while remaining useful to prospective clients.
3. Demonstrate technical breadth through specific, verifiable implementation details rather than unsupported claims.
4. Keep every contact path honest about its current behavior and make the next step unmistakable.
5. Preserve Nick's public identity, Nairobi location, broad software-development positioning, and confirmed project record across future surfaces.
